# Track data attribution

All track files here are generated by `modules/tracks/tools/build_tracks.py`
from the list in `modules/tracks/tools/tracks.json`.

- Centre lines, track widths and racing lines of the circuits with a
  `"tumftm"` entry come from the
  [TUMFTM racetrack-database](https://github.com/TUMFTM/racetrack-database)
  (Technical University of Munich, LGPL-3.0). Its centre lines originate from
  OpenStreetMap and its widths from satellite imagery.
- Pit lanes, and the centre lines of Mugello and Imola, come from
  [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright),
  © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence
  (ODbL).

The layouts are the real-world circuits. Circuit and car names are used as
the project owner chose; see "Naming and licensing" in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md.
