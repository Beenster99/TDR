# Trackside scenery attribution

The files here are generated by `modules/graphics/tools/build_scenery.py`
(see docs/GRAPHICS.md, "Real trackside scenery"). Each file lists its
sources under `"sources"`.

- Building footprints and names, grandstands, landuse, roads, paths, hedges,
  bridges and single trees come from
  [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright),
  © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence
  (ODbL).
- Building, bridge and tree heights and tree positions on English circuits
  come from the Environment Agency LIDAR Composite DSM 1 m,
  © Environment Agency copyright and/or database right, licensed under the
  [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).
- No satellite or aerial photographs (Google or otherwise) are used.
