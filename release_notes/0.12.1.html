Road class

Ten new road cars

Pick them in the Road class on the race setup or in the garage.

  • Mazda MX-5
  • Toyota GR Yaris
  • Honda Civic Type R
  • Alpine A110 S
  • BMW M2
  • Toyota GR Supra
  • Porsche 718 Cayman GT4 RS
  • Nissan GT-R Nismo
  • Chevrolet Corvette Z06
  • McLaren 750S

Each car has its real power, mass, dimensions and gearing. The 3D bodies were made with TripoSG (MIT licence) from one freely licensed Wikimedia photo of each car, then fitted to the car's real size.

Try it

  • Unzip the folder and double-click TopDownRacer.exe, as before.
  • Online, everyone should update to v0.12.1 so all the cars are there for everyone.
  • Race the MX-5 at Brands Hatch, then try the 750S at Spa.
  • Set up a multi-class race of GT3 against road cars.

Known limits

  • Online, handing a co-op car back from an AI co-driver to a player is still unreliable.
