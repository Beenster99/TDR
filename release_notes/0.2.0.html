AI
1 Nov
Drivers

AI drivers with personalities

The cautious placeholder AI is gone. 50 named drivers, each in a team with a number, age and style, race with eight traits: pace, consistency, aggression, overtaking, defending, tyre management, wet weather skill and composure.

  • They learn each track as they race: a corner where they slid or ran wide is taken a little slower next time, a clean one a little faster.
  • They pick a side and commit to a pass, cover the inside when you attack (one move, never under braking), leave you room when side by side, and move over when a leader laps them.
  • They make mistakes: braking too late or running wide, more often with a car right behind, in the wet or at night.
  • Tyre managers back off when their tyres overheat; wet weather specialists keep more pace in the rain.
  • The opening lap is calmer than before. In a 20-car GT3 test race at Mugello every car finished, and after lap 1 there were no contacts.
  • New AI difficulty setting: Rookie, Amateur, Pro (drivers as written), Expert or Alien. It changes how good every driver is but keeps their character.
  • Up to 24 AI cars in a race.
Race
1 Nov
Settings

Time of day and weather

The host now sets the conditions, and everyone online sees the same thing.

  • Start time (any hour) and clock speed (real time up to 60x, or frozen), so a race can run into the night.
  • Weather: dry, overcast, light rain, heavy rain or random, and whether it can change during the race.
  • Air and track temperature follow the sun, cloud and rain. Rain puts water on the track, the sun dries it, traffic clears a dry racing line first, and rubber builds up in the dry.
  • Grip depends on the water and your tyres, with aquaplaning on slicks in standing water. Water cools the tyres.
  • Tyres per player: auto (suits the weather), dry, intermediate or full wet. The AI choose for the start weather.
  • Grid order: AI ahead, random, or reversed (fastest at the back).
  • Dusk and night lighting, rain, mist, headlights and spray behind cars in the wet. The HUD shows the clock, weather, temperatures, track state and the next change.
Drive
1 Nov
Handling

Easier handling, sound and zoom

Everything from the v0.1.1 update after your first drive, plus a better zoom.

  • Driving help on the main menu: Full (stability assist, traction control, ABS), Some, or Off.
  • Sound made by the game itself: engines by revs and throttle, rivals around you, tyre squeal, grass rumble, crashes, start lights and lap chimes.
  • Free zoom from close up to the whole track, with the camera looking ahead along the track. Keyboard: Q and E (or the mouse wheel), Z or Tab for the whole track. Controller: hold LB or RB (or D-pad up and down), or use the right stick; click the right stick for the whole track.

Try it

  • Unzip the folder and double-click TopDownRacer.exe, as before.
  • Single race: GT3 at Mugello with 15 AI on Pro, and see who passes whom.
  • Set the start time to 19:00 with clock speed 60x and weather that changes, then race into the night and the rain.
  • Try AI difficulty Rookie and Alien to feel the difference.

Known limits

  • AI lap times are still about 10 to 20% slower than real ones, mostly because tyres run cooler than their best temperature in the physics.
  • The opening lap can still bring some bumping in a big pack.
  • No qualifying yet, and no pit stops or tyre changes (wave 3).
  • Driver traits aren't shown in the game yet.
