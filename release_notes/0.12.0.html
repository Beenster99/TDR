Race
5 Nov
Multi-class

Pick the classes in a multi-class race

Multi-class now asks which classes race: Le Mans (Hypercar, LMP2, GT3), WEC Hypercar against GT3, or any 2 or 3 classes.

  • The grid lines up class by class, and the choice is remembered, sent to guests and used by seasons.
  • Every class has its own colour and short name: chips on the timing tower, standings and gap panels, and roof number boards in class colours.
  • Results get a class column, class winners, a class filter, per-class gaps and points, and the best lap per class in purple.
  • On the pit wall, an ON THE ROAD strip and arrows say whether the cars ahead and behind are racing you or just traffic.
Tracks
5 Nov
Circuits

Tracks another 20% wider

Circuits are now 2.16 times their real width, and rallycross circuits and joker lanes 1.44 times their built width.

  • Derby arenas stay as they were, since wider meant too few crashes.
  • Side-by-side stretches still keep 10 m apart, pit lanes follow the new edge, and racing lines are worked out again.
Wall
5 Nov
Strategist

The strategist on one screen

Everything now fits on one screen: drivers table, onboard camera, feed, weather radar, your car's setup and the selected car.

  • Your car is drawn from above with a card per tyre for temperature, hot pressure and wear.
  • PIT STOP SETTINGS opens the planner in the middle. Cancel drops unsent changes, and Send to driver closes it.
Pits
5 Nov
Pit lane

You drive into the pit lane, with a release countdown

You now always drive into the pit lane yourself.

  • New default, "Drive to the pit limit": the guide takes over from the limit line to the box. "Drive the whole stop" keeps the full self-drive. The fully automatic option is gone.
  • The last 3 seconds of a stop count down in the middle of the screen (RELEASE 3, 2, 1, GO), and you get the car back as the crew let it go.
Co-op
5 Nov
Pit crew

Clearer co-op pit stops

The pit crew job is easier to follow.

  • A panel lists your job's steps with ticks, a gauge and a big "do this now" line, with buttons shown as key caps for the keyboard or pad you used last.
  • Nothing about the stop shows until the car is in the pits.
  • A glowing target ring and a trail of dots lead your crew member to the next thing to do.
  • The crew, wheels, wheel guns, fuel rig and tyre stacks are rebuilt in more detail, and draw faster.
Sound
5 Nov
Team radio

A real race engineer on the radio

The driver now hears pit wall messages as a natural race engineer's voice instead of the computer's voice, male or female.

  • The on-screen message box only appears when the voice is off.
  • Choose the voice in Settings > Sound > Radio voice. The computer voice stays as a choice.
Online
5 Nov
Pause

Escape pauses an online race for everyone

Any player pressing Escape now pauses the race on every machine and opens everyone's pause menu, so all can change settings.

  • Players who resume see who the race is waiting for. Once everyone has pressed Resume (or the host resumes for all), a 3 second countdown restarts the race.
Track
5 Nov
Graphics

Real asphalt and better rallycross dirt

Roads now look like real asphalt: stone in bitumen, paving joints, repair patches, crack sealing, tyre streaks and a rubbered racing line.

  • Gravel has real pebbles, and in the rain the road's pores fill with water first.
  • Rallycross dirt now frays into the tarmac with dust and loose stones, and tyres carry dirt out along the racing line.
Drive
5 Nov
Physics

Fair tyre wear and derby traction

A few seconds of hard sliding no longer cooks a tyre, so your tyres wear like the AI's.

  • Derby cars get much more traction on dirt, so they no longer crawl and spin in the hairpins.
  • The GT3 dash now holds the flag colour steady instead of flashing.

Try it

  • Unzip the folder and double-click TopDownRacer.exe, as before.
  • Online, everyone needs v0.12.0. Older games are turned away at the lobby.
  • Set up a Le Mans multi-class race with Hypercars, LMP2 and GT3.
  • Make a pit stop and watch for the release countdown.

Known limits

  • The 10 road cars are still being built and aren't in this release.
  • Online, handing a co-op car back from an AI co-driver to a player is still unreliable.
  • Co-op isn't available in Rallycross or Demolition Derby.
