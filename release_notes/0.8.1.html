Top Down Racer

In co-op, the whole team now works the pit stop. When the strategist calls the car in, everyone's camera closes in on the box and each player takes a job in the crew: fuel, windscreen, air jacks, wheel guns and wheels. The crew themselves are rebuilt, and you can see them pick up, carry and fit every tyre.

Pit stop

The team works the stop

The strategist calls the car in and the driver follows the guide line to the pit entry. From there the car drives itself down the lane and every player's camera zooms into the box.

  • First the strategist is the fuel man: carry the hose over, plug it in, and take it off when the tank has the planned fuel. Take it off early and you get less fuel.
  • At the same time the driver is the windscreen man.
  • Once the fuel is off, each player does the tyre job they picked in the lobby: left or right gun man, or left or right wheel man. Tyres only go on after the fuel.
  • Gun men pick up the gun, take the nut off, do it up again, then move to the rear wheel. Wheel men take the old wheel off, put it down, pick up the new one and fit it.
  • The AI does every other job. A planned driver change happens during the stop.
  • When the lollipop goes green the cameras pull out, the car drives itself to the end of the pit lane and hands back control there, and the strategist is back at the console.

Pit crew

A job for a third player

A third player in the team, in the pit crew role, gets their own part of the stop.

  • From the strategist's call they lay out the right tyres by each corner and a wheel gun on each side before the car arrives.
  • When the car stops they plug in the air lance that fires the jacks, then do the tyre job they picked.
  • In a two-player team the AI does all of this.
Menus
3 Nov
Lobby

Pick your pit job in the lobby

Each player's card in the co-op lobby has a Pit stop job picker: left or right gun man, or left or right wheel man. The strategist is always the fuel man.

  • Pit crew controls: move with the stick or WASD, use the wheel gun or cloth with Space, Enter or pad A, grab or put down with G, X or pad X. All of them can be rebound.
  • Settings > Gameplay > "Co-op pit stops: play your crew job" turns it off if your team would rather let the AI crew do it. It's on by default.
  • Single player keeps the wheel gun mini-game: you're the left gun man.

Graphics

Rebuilt pit crew figures

The crew are new jointed figures with knees, elbows, gloves, boots and helmets with visors.

  • You can see them carry and fit wheels, use the wheel guns, drag the fuel hose, wipe the screen and plug in the air lance.
  • New tyres are laid out with coloured compound bands, old ones are put down beside the car, and dry, inter and wet stacks stand in the garage.
  • A ring under a crew member shows who is played by you (yellow) and by a team mate (blue).

Try it

  • Unzip the folder and double-click TopDownRacer.exe, as before.
  • Online, everyone needs v0.8.1. v0.8.0 games are turned away at the lobby.
  • Start a co-op race with fuel on, pick a pit job each in the lobby, and have the strategist call the car in.
  • Try it with a third player in the pit crew role.

Known limits

  • Not yet tried on a real graphics card or with a real pad; tested with automatic players on one machine and over the network.
  • There are more pit crew figures now, about 13 a garage, which may cost frames on slower PCs.
  • Online, handing a co-op car back from an AI co-driver to a player is still unreliable.
  • Co-op isn't available in Rallycross or Demolition Derby.
