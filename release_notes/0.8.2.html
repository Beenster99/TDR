Top Down Racer

A round of fixes and changes from playing v0.8.1. The AI races fairly and starts two wide, and there's a pause menu, a handbrake, sharper steering in slow corners, a tidier HUD, and the game remembers your race settings. Rallycross loses its flags and safety car, marshals wait for a gap before going on track, Demolition Derby gets small arenas and ramps you can see, and the driving line can show the corners only.

AI
3 Nov
Racecraft

Fairer AI and proper starts

The AI no longer swerves across you, and the field gets off the line two wide.

  • The AI gives you room as soon as your nose is level with its rear wheels, and keeps that room through turn-in and exits instead of following its line into you.
  • Defending is one steady move, never across a car that has already committed to that side.
  • At the start each AI holds its grid lane until it brakes for turn 1, and follows nose to tail off the line.
  • Launch wheelspin: floor it off the line and the rears spin, even with traction control. Feather the throttle for the best start. Some aggressive AI drivers floor it too.
  • In AI tests, contacts fell by half and cars leaving the track by more than two thirds. Demolition Derby AI still rams you.
Menus
3 Nov
Pause

A pause menu

Esc, or Start on a pad, no longer quits straight to the main menu. It opens a pause menu.

  • Resume, Restart race, Settings, Quit to menu and Quit to desktop. Restarting and quitting ask you to press twice.
  • Offline the race pauses. Online it carries on for everyone, and your car gets no input while the menu is open.
  • Online, the host can restart the race for everyone or end it and send everyone back to the lobby. Guests can't restart, and nobody can in a championship.
  • Settings that change how the car drives are greyed out until the next race. The racing line, braking zones and pit lane guide can be switched mid-race.
Driving
3 Nov
Car control

Handbrake and sharper slow-speed steering

A handbrake in every race type, and the steering now has its full lock in hairpins.

  • Handbrake: Space, or A on a pad. It locks the rear wheels so the back swings round. Throttle no longer falls back to A, shift up moves to B, and the next tyres button moves to D-pad up. All rebindable.
  • Steering keeps full lock up to about 29 km/h, then eases off with speed, as before from about 200 km/h. Hairpins, the pit lane and turning round after a spin are much easier.
  • Tyre squeal is a rougher, lower rubber howl. Tyres no longer squeal on gravel, grass, dirt or mud; you hear the dirt scrabble instead.
HUD
3 Nov
Race screen

A tidier HUD

The race screen shows less in each panel and gives weather and fuel their own items.

  • Position and laps now shows only those two. Lap times are in the sector panel and the timing tower.
  • Tyres and grip is a compact 2 x 2 grid with grip on top.
  • New items: Time, weather and track (bottom right) and Fuel (bottom left, only with fuel on). Move, resize or hide them in the HUD editor (F2).
Menus
3 Nov
Race setup

Your race settings are remembered

The single race page and the online lobby start with the settings you last used: race type, track, car, laps, AI, weather, fuel, tyre wear and the rest.

  • Each race type remembers its own settings.
  • Seasons keep their own settings.
Race
3 Nov
Rallycross

Rallycross without flags

Rallycross has no yellow flags, virtual safety car, safety car or pit wall.

  • The joker lane shows on the lap map in orange with a "J", and has orange and white marker posts.
  • At Montalegre and Hell the joker lane sank under the grass on slopes. It now follows the ground.
Rules
3 Nov
Race control

Marshals wait for a gap

Marshals wait behind the barrier and only run on to clear debris when the next car is far enough away. They run off before it arrives.

  • On circuits the double yellows stay out from when the marshals are sent until they're back for good.
  • Retired cars are pushed behind the barrier in a gap.
  • Cars in the pits don't count as traffic.
Race
3 Nov
Derby

Demolition Derby, closer and messier

The derby changes you asked for after v0.8.1.

  • No pit stops or repairs. A wrecked car keeps going while its engine boils dry, about a minute of driving, then dies. A car that doesn't move for 15 seconds is out.
  • Every arena is about half its old size, with narrower roads. At most 16 cars, gridded two abreast.
  • Ramps show from above: yellow chevrons up the take-off, a hazard stripe on the lip, white chevrons down the landing. The kickers are steeper, and no ramp sits on dirt.
  • More oversteer on dirt: the tail comes round, most of all on rear-wheel drive cars.
  • No pit wall, flags, marshals, braking boards or kerbs in the arenas.
Aids
3 Nov
Driving line

Driving line: corners only

Settings > Gameplay > Driving line now has Full, Corners only and Off.

  • Corners only shows the line from just before each braking zone, through the apex and out of the corner, plus fast bends taken flat out. It fades out on the straights.
  • With braking zones on, a red "brake now" warning still shows anywhere you're too fast.
  • If you had the racing line off before, it stays off.
Pits
3 Nov
Fix

Single player pit stops release you again

With the solo pit crew mini-game on, your gun man started without his gun, so the crew waited for him before releasing the car.

  • He now starts with the gun in hand.
  • In any crew, the gun button next to a gun on the ground also picks it up.

Try it

  • Unzip the folder and double-click TopDownRacer.exe, as before.
  • Online, everyone needs v0.8.2. Older games are turned away at the lobby.
  • Press Esc in a race for the pause menu.
  • Spin the car round in a hairpin with the handbrake.

Known limits

  • The handbrake moved some pad buttons: shift up is now B and next tyres is D-pad up.
  • Online, handing a co-op car back from an AI co-driver to a player is still unreliable.
  • Co-op isn't available in Rallycross or Demolition Derby.
