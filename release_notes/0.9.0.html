Tracks
3 Nov
Circuits

Roads 50% wider

Every circuit's road is now one and a half times its real width, so there is room to race side by side. Rallycross and Demolition Derby are unchanged.

  • The inside of the tightest hairpins widens less, so they keep their shape.
  • Pit lanes moved out with the road edge, and the racing lines were worked out again for the wider roads.
Pits
3 Nov
Pit lane

A solid pit wall

On most circuits the pit lane sat on top of the main straight, so cars drove straight through the pit wall. Now there's a real wall between the track and the lane.

  • Pit lanes moved out until they sit clear of the track along the garages.
  • The wall is 1 m thick, with a debris fence on the track side, and it stops cars from either side like a concrete wall.
Graphics
3 Nov
Scenery

Sharp ground round every circuit

The fields, woods, roads, car parks and water round each circuit are now modelled, not painted on a low-resolution picture. They stay sharp at any zoom, even at 4K.

  • Stray lines of map gravel no longer show round the circuit.
Damage
3 Nov
Bodywork

Cars crumple like real cars

Damaged bodywork now crushes on every car, not just derby cars, and the dents can be seen from above.

  • Noses and tails fold like an accordion, with the bonnet or boot lid buckled up behind. An offset hit crushes that corner more.
  • Side hits cave the doors in and drag the roof rail down. A second hit somewhere else on the same side makes a second dent.
  • Paint darkens in the folds, and the lights, wings, splitters and glass bend with the body.
Pits
3 Nov
Co-op

Pit stops: the crew game is co-op only

In single player the AI crew now does the whole stop for you. In co-op, players still work the crew.

  • Your crew member is marked by a yellow hoop.
  • Tips name the button you have actually mapped, on the keyboard or the controller, whichever you used last.
  • With no fuel to put in, the fuel man stands by at the rig instead of vanishing.
  • A stop never runs past the crew's give-up time.
Race
3 Nov
After the flag

Watch the race after you finish, then a full results screen

Once you've finished or retired, you can watch the rest of the race. When every car is home, a detailed results screen fades in.

  • Pick any car (left and right, or click the running order) and any camera (C or Y): follow, chase, onboard, rear onboard, trackside TV or whole track.
  • Trackside TV uses real broadcast cameras on poles round the circuit. They pan and zoom to follow the car, then cut to the next one.
  • The results show positions, race time and gaps, best and average laps, best sectors (field bests in purple), best potential lap, pit stops, penalties with reasons, points, and the championship standings in a season.
Rules
3 Nov
Race control

Fewer yellow flags, and Safety car Off

Yellow flags now come out only for real incidents, and there's a new setting to turn flags off altogether.

  • Rubbing between cars no longer brings out a yellow flag. A real crash, a spun car, or a car crawling through the gravel still does.
  • Safety car setting: On, Yellow flags only, or Off (no flags and no safety car).
  • The flag board sits higher and is see-through.
  • The safety car is far easier to spot: a flashing roof light bar, corner strobes, amber light on the road, a flashing ring that grows as you zoom out, and an SC tag you can read with the whole track in view.
  • When the safety car is coming in this lap, the board turns green and tells you to get ready.
Rules
3 Nov
Penalties

The car you must let past is highlighted

When race control tells you to give a place back, the car you have to let past gets a pulsing outline and a LET PAST tag.

  • The race control box names that car and says how far ahead or behind it is. Its row on the timing tower turns orange, and an arrow at the edge of the screen points to it when it's out of view.
HUD
3 Nov
Timing tower

You stand out on the timing tower

Your row on the timing tower has a gold band. Other players online are shown in blue.

  • New option, Settings > Gameplay > Car highlights fade: never (the default), or after 5, 15, 30 or 60 seconds. The outlines come back at the green light and whenever you zoom.
Strategy
3 Nov
Pit wall

A real weather radar for the strategist

The pit wall radar now looks like a real rain radar, with forecasts.

  • Rain shows from light blue through green and yellow to red, on a map with range rings. Arrows show where each cell is heading, and lines mark rain 10, 20 and 30 minutes from the track.
  • Play it as a loop from 15 minutes ago to 30 minutes ahead, and zoom in.
  • The forecast is close but not perfect, and it gets better as the rain gets nearer. Each change says how long it will last and how sure the forecast is.
Strategy
3 Nov
Pit wall

Move, resize and zoom the pit wall panels

Drag a panel's title bar to move it. Drag its corner to zoom its contents from 60 to 200%, and its edges to resize it.

  • Panels snap to the screen edges and to each other. The layout is remembered, and Reset layout puts it back.
  • The highlight rings no longer show in the onboard picture.
Aids
3 Nov
Rallycross

Driving line through the joker

In rallycross, while you still owe your joker lap, a dashed branch of the driving line shows the way into the joker lane.

Race
3 Nov
Derby

Derby cars get off the line

Derby cars now have grip on dirt, so every car can get going from a standstill.

  • 0 to 40 km/h on dirt now takes about 4 seconds. Before it was over 5, and some cars never got going. The tails still step out.
  • Arenas are 50% bigger so the AI copes, and the road runs right up to the walls.
Menus
3 Nov
Controls

Button prompts show your own buttons

Every hint in the game now names the key or button you have mapped, on the device you used last (keyboard and mouse, or controller). PlayStation pads get Cross, L1 and so on.

  • Pad A and B now work as accept and back in every menu.
Menus
3 Nov
Quality of life

Smaller fixes

A few things that make the game nicer to use.

  • "Are you sure?" Yes / No popups for restarting, ending the race and quitting, in the pause menu and on the main menu. No is picked first.
  • The mouse pointer hides while you drive and comes back when you move the mouse.
  • The lobby fits on one screen at 720p and 1080p without scrolling.
  • The exhaust pops and crackles are gone. They sounded like constant banging.

Try it

  • Unzip the folder and double-click TopDownRacer.exe, as before.
  • Online, everyone needs v0.9.0. Older games are turned away at the lobby.
  • Finish a race and watch the rest of the field on the trackside TV cameras.
  • Try a race at Spa on the wider road, and look for the new pit wall.

Known limits

  • The pit crew game is co-op only now. In single player the AI crew does the stop.
  • Online, handing a co-op car back from an AI co-driver to a player is still unreliable.
  • Co-op isn't available in Rallycross or Demolition Derby.
