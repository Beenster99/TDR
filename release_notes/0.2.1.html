Elevation

Real hills on all 22 circuits

Heights come from the free elevation maps on AWS Open Data (NASA SRTM, USGS and national surveys), cleaned up so that trees and grandstands don't count as hills.

  • From lowest to highest point: Spa 104 m (real about 104), Red Bull Ring 63 m, Nürburgring 55 m, Mugello 44 m, Interlagos 43 m, Suzuka 43 m, Hungaroring 39 m, Brands Hatch 31 m, COTA 31 m.
  • Eau Rouge and Raidillon, Mugello's plunge to San Donato and the climb at COTA's turn 1 are all there.
  • The land around each circuit has its hills too, ready for the 3D view that's being built.
  • Albert Park couldn't be placed on the map, so it stays flat for now (it nearly is in real life).

Physics

Slopes and crests change how the car drives

The car feels gravity along the road and how hard it's pressed into it.

  • Going up a hill slows you down; going down one makes the car harder to stop.
  • Over a crest the car gets lighter and has less grip; in a dip or a compression it's pressed down and grips more.
  • Stopped on a hill, the car rolls back unless you hold the brake.
  • AI drivers know about it too: they brake earlier downhill and over crests.

HUD

Elevation strip on the HUD

Bottom middle of the screen: the whole lap's hills as a line, so you know what's coming.

  • Your car is the yellow dot; every other car is a dot in its colour.
  • Above it: your height above sea level, the lap's total climb, and the gradient under you (amber uphill, blue downhill when steeper than 3%).
  • In the coming 3D view the hills will also show as shading, faint contour lines on the grass and height lines across the road that bunch up where it's steep.

Try it

  • Unzip the folder and double-click TopDownRacer.exe, as before.
  • Race at Spa and watch the strip through Eau Rouge and up the Kemmel straight.
  • Try Mugello and brake for San Donato at the bottom of the hill.

Known limits

  • The view is still flat from above, so for now the hills show on the HUD strip only. The 3D track with shading and contour lines is next.
  • Monza and Hockenheim run through woods, which the maps read as about 5 m extra hill.
  • Banking is a gentle estimate from the hillside; real banked corners can be added by hand.
  • Online, everyone needs this version (older versions won't join).
