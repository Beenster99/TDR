Pit stops

Pit stops by the rules

Every stop now follows the real order and ends with a verdict.

  • Every car stops on its box marks. The guide brakes onto them and steers straight in, and if a car overshoots, the crew pushes it back.
  • The crew fuel the car and clean the windscreen first, then the air jacks go up, then the tyres, repairs, car down and release. In co-op the air lance player waits for the fuel too.
  • A timer in the middle of the screen shows the crew's current step and any problems (overshooting the box, a fumbled wheel, pit lane speeding, a slow player crew), then says whether the stop was clean.

Repairs

Fair repairs for everyone

The AI and players now repair by the same rule, so neither skips work the other's crew would do.

  • AI teams may leave light damage unrepaired to keep their track position, as real teams do.

Strategist

Damage advice for the strategist

When your car is damaged, a DAMAGE card replaces the setup on the strategist's OUR CAR panel.

  • It shows how bad the damage is, how much time it costs each lap, how long the repair would take, and whether to pit or stay out.
  • The same call appears under Repairs in the pit stop settings.
  • The numbers come from the pit stop's own repair times and lap time losses measured for each damaged part.

Try it

  • Unzip the folder and double-click TopDownRacer.exe, as before.
  • Online, everyone should update to v0.13.0 together.
  • Make a pit stop and watch the timer and the clean verdict.
  • Damage your car, then open the pit wall and read the damage card.

Known limits

  • Online, handing a co-op car back from an AI co-driver to a player is still unreliable.
  • Co-op isn't available in Rallycross or Demolition Derby.
