4 Nov
Results

Drive on after the flag, live results

You keep your car for 10 seconds after crossing the line. Then the results fade in while the race carries on behind them.

  • Cars already home are in place; the rest are dimmed as "racing" and each takes its row and lights up as it crosses the line.
  • Pick Watch the race (or the timing tower key) to spectate with the sound back, and press it again to return.
  • When the last car is home the live table becomes the final one, or the podium plays first.
Tyres
4 Nov
Pit wall

Tyre wear on each corner

Every car's four tyres now report their own wear.

  • The strategist's timing and pace tables show the rubber left on each tyre, and the pace table can sort on it.
  • The selected car shows each tyre with its wear rate and the laps left on the worst one.
  • The results driver page gets a wear column, a tyre wear graph and the wear at the end of each stint.
AI
4 Nov
Driving

AI crashes far less, and an AI mistakes setting

The AI now catches a car that starts to rotate under braking, brakes for the line it is really on, and keeps more room to the edge.

  • Attacks only go off line close to the car ahead, and give up when the room runs out.
  • Damaged AI cars drive to the grip they have left.
  • New race setting AI mistakes: Never, Rare, Normal, Often or Chaotic.
Rules
4 Nov
Race control

Stuck cars get fetched

An AI car beached in the gravel, wedged against the barrier or stopped on track now retires, so a crane or tractor comes for it.

  • Before, stuck cars sat parked for the rest of the race.
  • A recovery off the track runs under a virtual safety car; the real safety car is kept for a car on the track.
  • The safety car now drives the racing line and carries more speed through corners, so the pack isn't held up as much.
Speed
4 Nov
Performance

Smooth race starts

The frame rate no longer collapses at the start and on the pit straight.

  • The checks for the pit wall and pit lane now only look at the parts near each car, and wheels no longer cast their own shadows.
  • The race itself is unchanged.
Input
4 Nov
Controls

Clear any keybind

Right-click a binding, or select it and press Delete, Backspace or pad X, to clear it.

  • It saves straight away and the page says what the control still has. Hints for a control with nothing bound say "(not set)".
Cars
4 Nov
Graphics

New Porsche 911 GT3 R body

The 911 GT3 R has a new, more accurate body shape, fitted to the real wheelbase, with clean glass and painted lights.

Try it

  • Unzip the folder and double-click TopDownRacer.exe, as before.
  • Online, everyone needs v0.11.0. Older games are turned away at the lobby.
  • Finish a race and watch the results fill in, then pick Watch the race.
  • Set AI mistakes to Chaotic for a messy race, or Never for a clean one.

Known limits

  • Online, handing a co-op car back from an AI co-driver to a player is still unreliable.
  • Co-op isn't available in Rallycross or Demolition Derby.
