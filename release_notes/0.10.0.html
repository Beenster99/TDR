Podium
4 Nov
Celebration

Podium ceremony with champagne

Finish in the top three and the race fades to a podium: steps, a backdrop with the circuit name, mechanics in team colours, photographers, ticker tape and trophies.

  • Shake the bottle (right click, G, pad X or waggle the stick), pop the cork and spray (left click, Space, RT or A). Aim with the mouse, keys or stick.
  • The spray soaks whoever it hits. The AI drivers spray each other, you and the crowd.
  • Then on to the results. Turn it off in Settings > Gameplay > Podium celebration.
Results
4 Nov
Race data

Every driver's race, and race analysis

Click any driver on the results table to open their whole race.

  • Every lap: position, lap and sector times, gap to the leader, tyre, tyre temperature and fuel, plus a lap time graph against the winner, stints and pit stops.
  • Race analysis shows positions lap by lap, gaps to the leader, places won and lost from the grid and every tyre strategy.
  • The cars' sound fades away as the results come up.
Wall
4 Nov
Strategist

Pace table, rivals and a better pit stop plan

The strategist can now see who is quick right now, not just who is ahead.

  • One sortable pace table ranks every car on its last lap, last 3 and last 5 laps, with the gap to you. The fastest in each column is purple.
  • Mark a car as your rival (or let the game pick the next car of your class) and it shows orange. Backmarkers are tagged blue, and the next ones up the road are listed.
  • The pit stop plan is redrawn: the car in its colours, a card per tyre with compound, tread, temperature and pressure, and clear scales for the splitter and wing.
  • Fuel is set by the litre and shows how many laps it adds, in the pit stop plan, the co-op console and the single-player pit menu.
  • The rejoin band is now shown only to the strategist, not on the driver's screen.
AI
4 Nov
Driving

AI rejoins the track safely

An AI car that went off used to steer straight back to the racing line and cut across whoever was coming.

  • Now it waits beyond the kerb until the traffic behind has passed, comes back along the edge it left from, holds that edge until it is up to speed, then drifts back to the line.
  • Stuck cars no longer reverse into a car that is coming.
  • In a test of 30 offs over six tracks, contacts went from 9 to none.
  • A pass under yellow, VSC or safety car now has to be a clear gain before you are told to give the place back, and it never counts against a car in trouble.
HUD
4 Nov
Dash

GT3-style dash and clearer highlights

The speed and revs display now behaves like a real GT3 dash.

  • The whole dash flashes yellow for a local yellow, VSC or safety car, blue when a car that has lapped you is right behind, white for the chequered flag, and green briefly when the track goes clear.
  • The sectors bar shows the car behind on the left and the car in front on the right.
  • Car highlights are now a thin outline that stays the same width at every zoom, and kerbs and bumps no longer cut through them.
Sound
4 Nov
Engines

Better engine sounds

Every engine has been rebuilt with intake roar, exhaust resonance and combustion that varies slightly from one firing to the next.

  • Naturally aspirated engines have a loud intake; turbos are quieter.
  • The sound loop is twice as long, so you no longer hear it repeat.
  • Licensed recordings can now be dropped in to replace any engine later.
Track
4 Nov
Graphics

Smoother frame rate and grass to the kerb

The game draws less it doesn't need, so it runs more smoothly.

  • One shadow map from above instead of four, ground split into tiles so off-screen ground is skipped, and simple trees when you zoom far out. Small parts like lights and calipers no longer cast shadows.
  • The strip of old tarmac beside the kerb that drove like grass is gone. Grass now runs right up to the kerb; gravel traps and paved run-off are unchanged.
  • Pit crews wait inside the garage and come out only when their car is called in.
Menus
4 Nov
Interface

No more "are you sure"

Every menu action now happens on the first press.

  • Restart, End race, Quit to menu and Quit to desktop in the pause menu, Quit on the main menu, and the restore and reset buttons in Settings.

Try it

  • Unzip the folder and double-click TopDownRacer.exe, as before.
  • Online, everyone should update to v0.10.0 together.
  • Win a race (or finish third) and spray the champagne.
  • After a race, click a driver on the results to see their whole race.
  • Open the pit wall's pace table and mark a rival.

Known limits

  • The engine sounds are still made by the game; real recordings can be added later.
  • Online, handing a co-op car back from an AI co-driver to a player is still unreliable.
  • Co-op isn't available in Rallycross or Demolition Derby.
