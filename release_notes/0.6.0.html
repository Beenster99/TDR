Cars
2 Nov
Cars

Hypercars and LMP2

Two new classes, faster than the GT3s, built the same way: real-world dimensions turned into 3D models with parts for upgrades.

  • Hypercar: Porsche 963, Ferrari 499P, Toyota GR010 Hybrid, Cadillac V-Series.R and Peugeot 9X8.
  • LMP2: Oreca 07 Gibson and Ligier JS P217 Gibson.
  • Prototype shapes: pods over the wheels, canopy, shark fin, two-element rear wings, mirrors on the fenders.
  • Class order on track: Hypercar, then LMP2, then GT3. Prototypes corner at about 2.4 g at 220 km/h against 1.8 g for a GT3, and stop much shorter.
  • Each class has its own setup template and upgrade prices, and the garage sells them.
Looks
2 Nov
Graphics

Sponsors, numbers and more detail

Cars now look like race cars, and two cars of different makes no longer look the same.

  • 60 made-up sponsors placed on the bonnet, roof banner, rear deck, wing, end plates, doors, sills and haunches.
  • Race numbers on the doors.
  • Each car gets its own set of sponsors. In the garage, the new Sponsors option picks a package, a clean car, or new sponsors every race.
  • GT3 detail pass: each model has its own louvres, vents, intakes, wing mounts and wheel rims.
  • Colour schemes are spread out, so several copies of the same model on the grid look different.
Sound
2 Nov
Audio

Every engine sounds different

Engine sounds are built from each engine's layout: cross-plane and flat-plane V8, V10, V12, flat-six, straight-six, V6 and four, with or without a turbo.

  • Rev limiter at each car's redline, a cut and clack on upshifts, a blip on downshifts, pops and crackles off the throttle.
  • Turbo whistle and flutter, supercharger and hybrid whine, straight-cut gearbox whine.
  • Tyres squeal, scrub and lock up; you hear kerbs, gravel, grass, wind and rain spray.
  • Cars passing you change pitch (Doppler).

Aids
2 Nov
Gameplay

Driving aids

Each aid can be switched on or off in Settings > Gameplay.

  • Driving line: green where you can accelerate, yellow to lift and red to brake, plus numbered brake marker boards before the big stops.
  • Track assist holds a straight line and steers you gently back near the edge.
  • Manual gearbox: one gear per press. A downshift that would over-rev the engine is refused.
  • Less steering lock at speed, and slower keyboard steering at speed.
  • Fixed: AI cars behind the start line reversed 3 seconds after the green.
HUD
2 Nov
Race screen

Sector times and a timing tower

The bottom of the screen now shows this lap's sectors in purple, green or yellow, with the gap to the car ahead and behind.

  • Optional timing tower on the left: position, maker's badge, driver, last lap and gap. It fits a 45-car grid. Press H in a race, or set it in Settings.
  • Speed, temperatures and fuel use the units you pick in Settings.
FX
2 Nov
Graphics

Smoke, debris and pit crews

Sliding, spinning or locked tyres smoke and leave rubber marks. Off the road they kick up dust and tear up the grass.

  • Crashes throw carbon and paint, broken parts fly off and lie on the track, and contacts spark.
  • Each team's pit crew does the stop: lollipop, jacks, wheel guns, tyres, fuel hose and repairs. Every garage has people in it.
  • Fixed: cars disappeared into gravel traps on hilly tracks.
Menus
2 Nov
Front end

Intro, Settings and profile

A new intro screen with a GT3 pass-by, and a main menu with your profile badge in the top right.

  • Settings has five tabs: Gameplay, Graphics, Sound, Garage and Debug.
  • Power is shown in hp by default. You can also pick kW or PS, and units for speed, temperature, distance and fuel.
  • Cars you buy are kept in your garage, and saves keep a backup copy.
  • Grids go up to 25 cars in one class and 45 in multiclass.
  • Menus click like switches instead of beeping.

Try it

  • Unzip the folder and double-click TopDownRacer.exe, as before.
  • Set up a multiclass race at Donington with a big grid and the timing tower on. Watch the Hypercars lap the GT3s.
  • In the garage, try the Sponsors option on a Porsche 963.
  • Online, everyone needs v0.6.0.

Known limits

  • AI drivers ease off at about 280 km/h on long straights, so AI prototypes don't use all their speed. AI lap times are still about 12% off real ones.
  • The main menu shows the key art until a highlights video is added.
  • Pit stop times don't depend on how good the crew is yet.
