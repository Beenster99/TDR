TOP DOWN RACER  -  version 0.1.0  (1 November 2026)
===================================================

How to play: unzip this folder anywhere and double-click TopDownRacer.exe.
Nothing to install. If Windows says it protected your PC, press
"More info" then "Run anyway". The first time you host a race, Windows asks
about the firewall: tick both Private and Public networks.


WHAT'S NEW
----------

This is the first release: one real circuit, four sports cars, AI drivers
and online racing with friends.

- Spielberg GP Circuit. The real 4.3 km layout in Austria, from
  OpenStreetMap, with its kerbs, run-off and a two-wide grid. Laps are timed
  in three sectors, and cutting back across the line doesn't count.

- Four sports cars. The Flat-Six RS (a rear-engined flat-six coupe), the
  Berlinetta V8T (a mid-engined twin-turbo V8), the Grand Tourer V8 (a
  front-engined V8 GT) and the Featherweight S (a light supercharged
  roadster). Each has its own real-world weight, power, gearing,
  downforce and handling, so they drive differently.

- Tyres that warm up. Every tyre has its own temperature. They start
  cold and grip less, warm up as you push, and lose grip again if you
  overdo it. The outside tyres in a corner get hottest. The HUD shows all
  four (blue is cold, green is just right, red is too hot) and how much
  grip you have.

- Air and track temperature matter. Set them before the race. Hot air
  means a little less power and downforce, a cold track keeps the tyres
  cool, and a hot one warms them up.

- AI drivers. Fifteen named drivers, each with their own skill and
  consistency. They follow the racing line, brake for the corners, ease off
  on cold tyres, and steer round slower cars.

- Online races. Works the same way as Torque Shed: one player picks
  Host online race, the others type the host's address into the join
  box and pick Connect. The game uses UDP port 24690. The host's
  lobby shows exactly which address to give friends, and whether the
  router opened the port by itself. If it didn't, forward UDP 24690 to the
  host's PC by hand. Everyone drives their own car on their own PC, so
  there's no lag on your car. The host runs the AI cars and the timing. If
  someone drops out mid-race, the AI takes their car home.

- Controller from the start. RT throttle, LT brake, left stick to steer
  (A and X work as throttle and brake on pads without triggers). Y rotates
  the camera, Back restarts and Start goes to the menu. Every menu and the
  lobby work with the pad: D-pad or stick to move, A to pick, B to go back,
  left and right to change a setting. Keyboard: W A S D or the arrows, C for
  the camera, R to restart, Esc for the menu.

- Race settings. Laps (1 to 50), number of AI drivers (up to 15), air
  temperature, track temperature and time of day. In an online race the
  host picks them.


KNOWN ISSUES
------------

- The AI is careful: about ten seconds a lap slower than the real cars would
  be. Faster, braver AI with overtaking comes next.
- You can't join a race that has already started yet. Wait in the lobby for
  the next one.
- Weather, damage, fuel and pit stops, the safety car, upgrades, tuning and
  seasons aren't in yet. They're coming in the next updates.
