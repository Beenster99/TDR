Race rules

Change only the tyres you need

The next stop can change all four tyres, the fronts, the rears, the left or right side, or a single tyre.

  • Four tyres take 6 s, a pair about 3.8 s and one tyre 2.6 s.
  • A change to a different compound always fits all four. A flat tyre is always changed when repairs are on.
  • Press Y in a race, or LB / RB on the pit wall screen with a controller. The pit crew console has a "Change" row too.
  • The pit crew only works on the wheels being changed.
  • AI teams sometimes change just the worn pair.

Race rules

Driver swaps at a pit stop

A new race setting, Drivers per car, goes from 1 to 3 (default 2).

  • Racing alone, you get an AI co-driver. Press N (or Y on the pit wall with a controller) and they take over at your next stop.
  • While they drive, you run the pit wall: call the stop, pick tyres and fuel, and take the wheel back at a later stop.
  • Online, the host's AI drives your car during the co-driver's stint.
  • AI cars share the car between two or three drivers and swap at their stops. The new driver's own pace and racecraft take over.
  • The timing tower shows who's driving at the moment. Results stay under the car's lead driver.
  • Co-op crews still swap between themselves.
Controls
2 Nov
Settings

Keybinds page

Settings > Controller lists every control, each with two keyboard keys and two controller buttons.

  • Pick a binding and press the new key or button. Backspace clears it and Esc cancels.
  • Clashes are flagged, and you can reset everything to the defaults.
  • You can set the stick dead zone.
  • Fixed: a guest's car always pulled to the right and zoomed out. Only the controller you're using now drives the sticks and triggers.

Class filter and remembered settings

Smaller fixes from your list.

  • Car class filter on the race pages, and a Class filter in the garage (including My garage).
  • Your driver name is saved as you type. Change it from "Profile and driver name" on the main menu.
  • The join page remembers the last host address, and Enter connects.
  • The left arrow on menu options now steps back.

Try it

  • Unzip the folder and double-click TopDownRacer.exe, as before.
  • Start a single race with Drivers per car on 2 and a mandatory stop. Press N, box, and watch your co-driver from the pit wall.
  • With tyre wear on, press Y to change only the fronts and compare the stop time.
  • Online, everyone needs v0.7.0.

Known limits

  • A car runs one compound on all four tyres: there are no mixed sets.
  • The co-driver change prompt on the HUD always names N, even after you rebind it.
  • AI drivers ease off at about 280 km/h on long straights, and AI lap times are still about 12% off real ones.
  • Pit stop times don't depend on how good the crew is yet.
