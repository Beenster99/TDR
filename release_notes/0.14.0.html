New bodies for every race car

All the GT3, LMP2 and Hypercar models have new 3D bodies, made with TripoSG (MIT licence) from freely licensed photos of the real cars and fitted to their real dimensions.

  • Hypercars: Porsche 963, Ferrari 499P, Cadillac V-Series.R, Toyota GR010 Hybrid and Peugeot 9X8.
  • LMP2: Oreca 07 and Ligier JS P217.
  • GT3: BMW M4, Ferrari 296, Lamborghini Huracán Evo2, McLaren 720S, Mercedes-AMG and Porsche 911 GT3 R.
  • Headlights get a proper lens look, and the glass is cut cleanly.

Arenas

Demolition Derby, round 5

Derby races are cleaner at the start and fairer through the crossovers.

  • On dirt the back of the car slides less and digs in harder for drive.
  • No grid sits on a crossover any more, and there's no ramming for the first 120 m, so the field leaves bumper to bumper.
  • At a crossover the AI keeps to its own road instead of turning onto the other one.
  • Two new arenas: Claypit Dirt Oval and Gasworks Tarmac Oval. Scrapyard Knot is gone.
  • The driving line is always off in a derby, since there's no best way round, only the car in front.

Try it

  • Unzip the folder and double-click TopDownRacer.exe, as before.
  • Online, everyone should update to v0.14.0 together.
  • Zoom in close on a Le Mans grid to see the new bodies.
  • Try a derby on Claypit Dirt Oval.

Known limits

  • Online, handing a co-op car back from an AI co-driver to a player is still unreliable.
  • Co-op isn't available in Rallycross or Demolition Derby.
