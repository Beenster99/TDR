Release notes2 Nov 2026v0.8.0New race types, pit stops and scenery

Top Down Racer

The biggest update yet. Two new race types, Rallycross and Demolition Derby, join circuit racing. Pit stops are now played out by a full crew, Le Mans style, and you can drive into your own box with a guide line. The strategist gets a pit planner and an onboard camera. Every circuit has its real trackside buildings and trees, the AI follows a proper fastest racing line, and you can rearrange the HUD.

2 Nov
Rallycross

Rallycross

Pick Rallycross as the race type in the race setup. Mixed tarmac and dirt circuits, short races and one joker lap each.

  • Five FIA World RX circuits built from OpenStreetMap with their real hills: Höljes, Montalegre, Hell, Lohéac and Estering.
  • Seven RX1 supercars modelled from real dimensions: Polo R, 208 WRX, S1 EKS, Fiesta ST, i20, Fabia and Mégane RS.
  • Dirt has less grip and more drag, and throws up dust and mud.
  • Take the joker lane once a race. It shows on the map and the HUD, and a missed joker costs 30 seconds. The AI plans when to take its own.
  • No pit stops, fuel, tyre wear, co-op or safety car in rallycross.

Derby

Demolition Derby

The third race type. Smash the others until yours is the last car still running.

  • Five made-up arenas with crossovers, table-top jumps and dirt.
  • 20 made-up derby cars. Bodies crumple where they're hit, windscreens crack and shatter, bonnets and boot lids tear off, and parts stay on the track.
  • Cars fly off the jumps. Steam, smoke, fire and soot show how hurt a car is, and wrecked cars retire.
  • Tougher cars take less damage. The AI rams you.
  • Damage is always full; no pit stops, flags, fuel, upgrades or setups.

Pit crews

Pit stops worked by a real crew

Every stop now runs like a Le Mans stop: the car controller, refueller, air jacks, tyre changers, the release. The stop takes as long as the crew does.

  • Each team's crew is a little faster or slower, jobs vary from stop to stop, and now and then a wheel gets fumbled. The HUD says how your stop compared with a standard one.
  • Tyres only go on once the fuel is in, as in the WEC.
  • "Two guns": in co-op, the pit crew player can work the wheel guns in a mini-game.
  • A band on the circuit, the minimap and the pit panel shows where you would rejoin among the traffic after a clean stop.
Pits
2 Nov
Pit lane

Drive into your own pit box

You now drive your own stop: brake for the limit line, hold the limiter and stop on your box marks.

  • A dashed guide line, coloured by speed, leads you down the pit lane, with the limit lines and your box outlined.
  • Overshooting the box costs time. Miss it and you go round again.
  • Single player: a pit call menu picks tyres (and which ones), splitter, rear wing, fuel and repairs, and pauses the race while you choose.
  • Turn the pit lane guide and the pit call menu on or off in Settings > Gameplay.
Wall
2 Nov
Strategist

Pit planner and onboard camera

The strategist plans the whole next stop on a plan view of the car and sends it in one go.

  • Set each tyre's compound and cold pressure on its own, front and rear downforce, fuel, repairs, a driver change and the box call.
  • Send gives the crew the plan and radios it to the driver, who sees it on screen and hears it read out over a crackly radio. Radio volume and read-out are in Settings > Sound.
  • A live onboard camera, front or rear, on any car, shows top right of the pit wall.
  • The crew console, strategist screen and team radio now work fully with mouse and keyboard.
Tracks
2 Nov
Scenery

Real trackside at every circuit

Buildings, named grandstands with crowds, roads, paths, hedges, bridges and woods now come from OpenStreetMap. English circuits also use Environment Agency laser scans for real building heights and the trees where they actually stand.

  • Silverstone came first, then 27 more circuits.
  • Forest tracks have up to about 150,000 trees.
  • Settings > Graphics > Trackside switches between Real and Simple for slower PCs.
  • The contour lines on the grass and the height lines across the road are gone. Hills still show through shading, sun shadows, the camera tilt and the HUD height strip.
AI
2 Nov
Racing lines

Proper racing lines

Every track has a new racing line worked out for the fastest GT3 lap: late apexes before straights, compromises through sequences, kerbs used.

  • The old lines on some tracks hugged the inside of every corner.
  • The AI follows the new line smoothly and slows properly for the second part of a chicane.
Rules
2 Nov
Race control

Marshals, recovery trucks and gravel

Race control now cleans up after incidents.

  • A big debris pile sends out four marshals with brooms under double yellow flags: no overtaking, slow down, or take a 5 second penalty.
  • A car retired on the circuit brings out the safety car and a recovery vehicle: a crane lifts a car out of the wall onto its truck, a tractor tows one away through a marshals' gate.
  • A car stopped in the gravel can now crawl out. It still digs in hard at speed.
Menus
2 Nov
Interface

HUD editor and a new race setup screen

Lay out the race screen the way you want it, and set up races on one screen.

  • HUD editor: press F2 in a race, or Settings > Gameplay > Edit HUD layout. Drag to move, resize from a corner, right-click to hide, tick to bring back, undo and reset.
  • Co-op drivers get their own minimal layout (speed, pit guide, lights, flags, radio), with its own switch in the editor.
  • Single race, lobby and season setup now fit on one screen: a circuit card with its map and description, a car card with your car turning on a stand, and the race settings.
  • The lobby shows a card per driver with READY / NOT READY, their car, what they're doing and their ping. Guests see the host's changes light up straight away.

Try it

  • Unzip the folder and double-click TopDownRacer.exe, as before.
  • Online, everyone needs v0.8.0. Older games are turned away at the lobby.
  • Change the race type to Rallycross and try Höljes, then to Demolition Derby.
  • Pit at Silverstone and drive the guide line into your box, then zoom out to see the real trackside.
  • Press F2 in a race and move the HUD around.

Known limits

  • Online, handing a co-op car back from an AI co-driver to a player is unreliable; one of our online tests fails on it.
  • Albert Park has no map position yet, so it keeps the simple trackside.
  • Real trackside with many trees is heavy on slower PCs; switch Trackside to Simple if it stutters.
  • Co-op isn't available in Rallycross or Demolition Derby.
